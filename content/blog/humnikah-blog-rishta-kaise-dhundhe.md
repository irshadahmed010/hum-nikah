## Pehle apna biodata banayein

Rishta dhundhne ka sabse pehla aur buniyadi step hota hai ek saaf-suthra aur sacha marriage biodata banana. Jab tak aapka biodata tayyar nahi hoga, doosri family tak aapki baat theek se nahi pahunch payegi.

Ek acche biodata mein yeh baatein zaroor honi chahiye:
- **Personal details:** Poora naam, umar, height, aur marital status (never married, divorced, ya widowed).
- **Taleem aur profession:** Degree ka naam, job ya business ki information, aur working location (jaise Bangalore, Mumbai, ya Gulf).
- **Family background:** Walidain ka occupation, bhai-behno ki tafseel, aur native place (abayi watan).
- **Partner expectations:** Aapko kaisa jeevansathi chahiye—taleem, nature aur values ke mutabiq—ise aasan aur shaishta alfaaz mein likhein.

Biodata mein fauran bank details ya ghar ka exact door number na daalein. Jab families aapas mein baat aage badhayein, tab yeh maloomat share ki ja sakti hain.

## Rishtedaar aur jaan-pehchaan

Tradition ke mutabiq rishtedaaron aur qareebi doston ke zariye rishta talash karna hamesha se sabse bharosemand tareeqa raha hai. 

Iske fayde yeh hain:
- Family background aur khandaan ki pehle se samajh hoti hai.
- Aapas mein mutual references hote hain jo tasdeeq mein madad karte hain.
- Initial baat-cheet mein jhijhak kam hoti hai.

Lekin sirf rishtedaaron par depend rehne ki ek limit hoti hai. Kabhi-kabhi specific career expectations, age preferences ya location ki wajah se family circle mein matches nahi milte. Aise waqt mein professional aur verified matchmaking bureau ka sahara lena ek behtar rasta sabit hota hai.

## Online profiles se bachke

Aaj kal internet aur mobile apps par bohot si matrimonial websites मौजूद hain. Inka istemal zaroor karein, magar poori hoshiyari ke saath:

- **Open WhatsApp groups se bachein:** Open groups mein apna ya apne bacchon ka biodata aur phone number kabhi mat daalein. Aise groups se numbers leak hote hain aur data misuse hone ka khatra rehta hai.
- **Pehle elders ko aage karein:** Online kisi se bhi baat shuru ho, toh pehle walidain aur elders ko aapas mein baat karne dein. Direct akele milna ya baatein karna galat fehmiyon ko janam deta hai.
- **Financial details kabhi na dein:** Agar koi shakhs shadi ke naam par paise maange, ticket ke kharche maange ya bank details maange, toh fauran contact khatam karein.

## Rishta verify kaise karein

Shadi zindagi bhar ka sath hai, isliye emotions mein aakar jald-bazi mein faisla na lein. Har rishtey ki basic verification zaroori hai.

In cheezon ki tasdeeq zaroor karein:
- **Identity aur address:** Government ID (Aadhaar, Passport) aur residential proof zaroor dekhein.
- **Job aur business:** Company ka naam, working status aur business ka physical office ya shop confirm karein.
- **Mohalla aur community enquiries:** Local masjid ke zariye, padosiyon se ya mutual jaan-pehchaan walon se family ki reputation ke baare mein daryaft karein.
- **Marital history:** Agar koi talaq-shuda ya bewa hai, toh legal aur shari documentation clear dekhein.
