## Biodata mein kya likhein

Ek standard Muslim marriage biodata mein yeh chaar main sections hone chahiye:

### 1. Zaati Maloomat (Personal Details)
- **Poora naam:** Jo documents par darj ho.
- **Date of birth aur umar:** Wazeh tarikh aur saal.
- **Height:** Jaise 5'5" ya 5'10".
- **Marital status:** Never married, divorced, ya widowed.
- **Mother tongue (zuban):** Urdu, Kannada, Malayalam, Tamil, etc.
- **Deeni maloomat:** Sunni Hanafi, Shafi'i, ya Ahle Hadees (agar aap mention karna chahein).

### 2. Taleem aur Kaam (Education & Profession)
- **Highest qualification:** Jaise B.Tech, MBA, MBBS, B.Com, ya BCA.
- **College ya university:** Institue ka naam.
- **Current job ya business:** Company ka naam, designation (role), aur city (jaise Bangalore, Hyderabad, ya Dubai).
- **Annual income (optional):** Agar aap likhna munasib samjhein, toh general range likh sakte hain.

### 3. Khandaan ki Maloomat (Family Background)
- **Walid ka naam aur occupation:** Business, private service, ya retired.
- **Walida ka naam aur background:** Homemaker ya working professional.
- **Bhai aur behan:** Unki tadaad, taleem aur marital status (jaise: 1 elder brother married, 1 younger sister studying).
- **Native place:** Aapka abayi watan ya hometown.

### 4. Jeevansathi ki Ummeed (Partner Expectations)
- Age group aur height preference.
- Taleem aur career expectation (kya ladki ka job karna zaroori hai ya optional).
- Location preference (kya family Bangalore ya kisi specific shahr mein rishta chahti hai).

## Kya baatein biodata se bahar rakhein

Shuruati stage par kuch baatein biodata mein share karna theek nahi hota:
- **Bank details ya salary slips:** Shuru mein bank balance ya account number kabhi na likhein.
- **Ghar ka exact door number:** Sirf locality aur shahr likhein (jaise: Cox Town, Bangalore ya Malakpet, Hyderabad).
- **Excessive technical skills:** Job resume ki tarah programming languages, project tools ya school marksheet daalne ki zaroorat nahi hai.
- **Guroor ya sakht shartein:** Aise alfaaz na likhein jo dusri family ko hurt karein. Expectations hamesha narmi aur izzat se likhein.

## Aam galtiyan jo log karte hain

In galtiyon se bachein taaki aapka rishta process aage badh sake:
1. **Lamba biodata banana:** Do ya teen page ka biodata koi nahi padhta. Sabhi zaroori baatein ek hi page par concise tareeqe se aani chahiye.
2. **Purani ya blurred photos lagana:** Agar photo laga rahe hain, toh recent, formal aur saaf picture lagayein. Filter wali ya group photos na lagayein.
3. **Galat ya exaggerated details:** Jo haqeeqat hai wahi likhein. Agar qualification ya job status ke baare mein galat likha gaya, toh baad mein baat toot jaati hai aur dukh hota hai.
4. **Wali ka contact na hona:** Ladki ke biodata mein hamesha walid, bhai ya guardian ka contact number hona chahiye, taaki baat shuru se izzat ke sath elders ke zariye ho.
